how it grows
Every exchange feeds it: words it hears count most, words it speaks half as much. Levels follow a square-root curve, so early growth is fast and later growth is earned. Size and detail follow level, colour warms or cools with the tone of what you say, and energy fades when you are away. Every few turns it reflects with the chat model and stores what it learned about you.
